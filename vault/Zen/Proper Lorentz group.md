# Proper Lorentz group

The **proper Lorentz group** $L_{+} = \mathrm{SO}(3,1)$ is the group of all [Lorentz transformations](Lorentz%20group.md) preserving the orientation of space, #m/def/group/lorentz
i.e.
$$
\begin{align*}
\mathrm{SO}(3,1) &= \{ \Lambda \in \mathrm{O}(3,1) : \det \Lambda = 1 \} 
\end{align*}
$$
where $\mathrm{O}(3,1)$ is the [Lorentz group](Lorentz%20group.md).
As the [kernel](Kernel%20of%20a%20group%20homomorphism.md) of the map $\det : \mathrm{O}(3,1) \to \mathbb{Z}_{2}$ it is a [normal subgroup](Normal%20subgroup.md) of $\mathrm{O}(3,1)$.
