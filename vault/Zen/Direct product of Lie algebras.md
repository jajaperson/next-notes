# Direct product of Lie algebras

The **direct product** is the [categorical product](Products%20and%20coproducts.md) in the *Category of Lie algebras*.
Given two Lie algebras $\mathfrak{g}, \mathfrak{h}$,
their product $\mathfrak{g} \times \mathfrak{h}$ consists of the *direct product vector space*[^sum] together with a bracket given by
$$
\begin{align*}
[(g_{1},h_{1}),(g_{2},h_{2})] = ([g_{1},g_{2}],[h_{1},h_{2}])
\end{align*}
$$

Hence regarded as subspaces $\mathfrak{g} \cong (\mathfrak{g}, 0)$ and $\mathfrak{h} \cong (0, \mathfrak{h})$ commute and are [ideals](Lie%20algebra%20ideal.md).

[^sum]: for two (or by induction, finite) operands this is naturally isomorphic to the [direct sum of vector spaces](Direct%20sum%20of%20vector%20spaces.md) $\mathfrak{g} \oplus \mathfrak{h}$
