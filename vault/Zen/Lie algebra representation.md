# Lie algebra representation

A **representation** $\pi : \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{gl}(V)$ of a [Lie algebra](Lie%20algebra.md) $\mathfrak{g}$ is a [Lie algebra homomorphism](Lie%20algebra%20homomorphism.md) from $\mathfrak{g}$ to the [commutator](Commutator.md) algebra of the [Endomorphism ring](Endomorphism%20ring.md) of $V$. #m/def/lie
Thus for any $X,Y \in \mathfrak{g}$,
$$
\begin{align*}
\pi([X,Y]) = \pi(X)\pi(Y) - \pi(Y)\pi(X)
\end{align*}
$$
A space $V$ carrying a representation of $\mathfrak{g}$ is sometimes called a $\mathfrak{g}$-[module](Module%20over%20a%20Lie%20algebra.md),[^1988] though this is an abuse of terminology.

[^1988]: 1988. [Vertex operator algebras and the Monster](../Sources/@frenkelVertexOperatorAlgebras1988.md), p. 5

## Properties

* Every Lie algebra has a natural representation on itself, the [adjoint Lie algebra representation](Adjoint%20Lie%20algebra%20representation.md).
* Every representation $\Gamma$ of a [Lie group](Lie%20group.md) induces an *Infinitesimal representation* $d\Gamma$ of the corresponding Lie algebra.
