# Module over a Lie algebra

A **module over a Lie algebra** $\mathfrak{g}$ or $\mathfrak{g}$-**module** is an abuse of terminology for a vector space carrying a [Lie algebra representation](Lie%20algebra%20representation.md).[^frenk] #m/def/lie
The actual [module](Module.md) being invoked is one over the [Universal enveloping algebra](Universal%20enveloping%20algebra.md) of the [Lie algebra](Lie%20algebra.md).

[^frenk]: See 1988, [Vertex operator algebras and the Monster](../Sources/@frenkelVertexOperatorAlgebras1988.md), pp. 5–6 for an example of this usage.
