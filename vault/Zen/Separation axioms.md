# Separation axioms

The **separation axioms**[^Trennungsaxiome] are a series of optional [properties](Topological%20property.md) for topological spaces,
ordered by [strength](Strong%20and%20weak%20conditions.md).

* $\mathrm{T}_{1}$ ::: [Fréchet space](Fr%C3%A9chet%20space.md)
* $\mathrm{T}_{2}$ ::: [Hausdorff space](Hausdorff%20space.md)
* $\mathrm{T}_{4}$ ::: [Normal space](Normal%20space.md) (and Hausdorff)

[^Trennungsaxiome]: German *Trennungsaxiome*, whence their symbols $\mathrm{T}_{n}$.
