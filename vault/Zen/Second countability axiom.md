# Second countability axiom

A topological space $(X, \mathcal{T})$ is called **second-countable** iff it has a countable [Topological basis](Topological%20basis.md). #m/def/topology

## Properties

* [Sequential compactness](Sequentially%20compact%20space.md) and [compactness](Compact%20space.md) are equivalent in a second-countable space.
* [Second countable implies Lindelöf](Second%20countable%20implies%20Lindelo%CC%88f.md)

## Examples

* [Real coördinate space](Real%20co%C3%B6rdinate%20space.md) with the usual topology has a countable basis given by balls with rational centres and rational radii.
