# Kernel of a Lie algebra homomorphism

The **kernel** $\ker f$ of a [Lie algebra homomorphism](Lie%20algebra%20homomorphism.md) $f : \mathfrak{ g} \to \mathfrak{h}$ over a field $\mathbb{K}$ is a special case of the [kernel of an algebra homomorphism](Kernel%20of%20an%20algebra%20homomorphism.md), #m/def/lie
i.e. $\ker f = f^{-1} \{ 0 \}$.
It follows from the general case that the kernel is necessarily a [Lie algebra ideal](Lie%20algebra%20ideal.md) of $\mathfrak{ g}$.
