# Maximal ideal

A (two-sided) proper [ideal](Ideal.md) $I \triangleleft R$ is called a **maximal ideal** iff whenever $J \trianglelefteq R$ is an [ideal](Ideal.md) such that $I \trianglelefteq J \trianglelefteq R$,
then either $I = J$ or $J = R$. #m/def/ring
$$
\begin{align*}
I \trianglelefteq J \trianglelefteq R \iff [I = J] \lor [J = R]
\end{align*}
$$
