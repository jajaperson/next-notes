# Simple Lie algebra

A [Lie algebra](Lie%20algebra.md) $\mathfrak{g}$ is said to be **simple** iff it is nontrivial, $\dim \mathfrak{g} > 1$, and it has no nontrivial [Lie algebra ideal](Lie%20algebra%20ideal.md) #m/def/lie
(equivalently the [adjoint Lie algebra representation](Adjoint%20Lie%20algebra%20representation.md) is irreducible).
