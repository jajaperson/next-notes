# Algebra over a field

An **algebra** $(V, \cdot)$ over a field $\mathbb{K}$ is a [Vector space](Vector%20space.md) $V$ over $\mathbb{K}$ equipped with a [bilinear](Multilinear%20map.md) product $(\cdot): V \times V \to V$, #m/def/falg
i.e. for any $x,y,z \in V$ and $a,b,c \in \mathbb{K}$

1. $(x+y)z = xz + yz$ ^A1
1. $z(x+y) = zx + zy$ ^A2
1. $(ax)(by)=(ab)(xy)$ ^A3

This may be generalized to an [Algebra over a commutative ring](Algebra%20over%20a%20commutative%20ring.md).
