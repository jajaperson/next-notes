# Irrep

An **irreducible representation** or **irrep** is a representation that is not [reducible](Reducibility%20of%20representations.md).
They play a fundamental role in representation theory, since  in many cases a representation may be decomposed into irreducible representations ([Maschke's theorem](Maschke's%20theorem.md)).

For a given group $G$, the set of (equivalence classes of) irreps is $\widehat{G}$, which is called the dual object.
If $G$ is abelian, then $\widehat{G}$ is a group (see [1-dimensional irrep](1-dimensional%20irrep.md)).
It is common to take a [unitary](Unitary%20representation.md) irrep as a specimen of each equivalence class.
