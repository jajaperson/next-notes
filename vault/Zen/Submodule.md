# Submodule

Let $(M, R, +, \cdot)$ be a (left) [module](Module.md).
A **submodule** $N \leq M$ is a module under the same operations, #m/def/module
i.e. $(N, +)$ is a [subgroup](Subgroup.md) such that $r \cdot n \in N$ for any $n \in N$ and $r \in R$.
Thus a submodule is an **invariant subspace** under the carried representation of $R$ (see [invariant subspace](Invariant%20subspace.md)).
