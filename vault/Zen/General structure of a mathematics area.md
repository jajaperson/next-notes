# General structure of a mathematics area

It is typical that an area of mathematics busies itself with a specific [category](Category.md),
therefore an MOC for subarea of maths should generally have the following structure.

1. **Fundamentals** — Outlines the *object of study* and some basis properties of it.
1. **Morphisms** — How these objects relate to each other
1. **Internally** — Things constructed “inside” the object, e.g. subsets of a space.
1. **Externally** — The creation of objects from other objects, usually by category-theoretic constructions.
