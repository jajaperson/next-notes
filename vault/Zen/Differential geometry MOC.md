# Differential geometry MOC

**Differential geometry** studies [smooth](Differentiability.md) geometric structures, in particular [differentiable manifolds](Differentiable%20manifold.md).
As such it is closely related to differential topology.
A guiding principle is the [Linearization dogma](Linearization%20dogma.md).

## Milnor's approach

*John Willard Milnor* and *Lyle Noakes* take an approach where only [real embedded manifolds](Real%20embedded%20manifold.md) are considered, as justified by the [Whitney embedding theorem](Whitney%20embedding%20theorem.md).

* [Differentiability on arbitrary subsets of real coördinate space](Differentiability.md#arbitrary-subsets-of-real-coordinate-space)
* [Tangent space](Tangent%20space.md#real-embedded-manifold)
* [Differential pushforward](Differential%20pushforward.md#real-embedded-manifold)
