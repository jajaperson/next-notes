# History of Category Theory

The following is a brief history of category theory, given by *Steve Awodey*[^2010]

* **1945**
  *Samuel Eilenberg* and *Saunders Mac Lane* originate the theory in their paper [General theory of natural equivalences](../Sources/@eilenbergGeneralTheoryNatural1945.md).
* **Late 1940s**
  The main applications were originally in the fields of *Algebraic Topology*,
  particularly homology theory, and [Abstract algebra MOC](Abstract%20algebra%20MOC.md).
* **1950s**
  *Alexander Grothendieck* and others begin applying category theory to *Algebraic Geometry*.
* **1950s**
  *F. William Lawvere* and others begin applying category theory to [Logic MOC](Logic%20MOC.md)
* **1970s**
  Applications begin to appear in computer science, linguistics, cognitive science, philosophy,
  and many others.

[^2010]: 2010. [Category theory](../Sources/@awodeyCategoryTheory2010.md)
