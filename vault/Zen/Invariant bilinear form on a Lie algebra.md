# Invariant bilinear form on a Lie algebra

A [bilinear form](Bilinear%20form.md) $\langle \cdot,\cdot \rangle : \mathfrak{g} \times \mathfrak{ g} \to \mathbb{K}$ on a [Lie algebra](Lie%20algebra.md) $\mathfrak{g}$ is said to be **invariant** iff the following equivalent conditions hold for any $x,y,z \in \mathfrak{g}$[^1988]

* $\langle [x,y],z \rangle = \langle x,[y,z] \rangle$

* $\langle \ad_{x} y, z \rangle + \langle y, \ad_{x}z \rangle = 0$

* $\langle [x,y], z \rangle + \langle y,[x,z] \rangle = 0$
  
  [^1988]: 1988. [Vertex operator algebras and the Monster](../Sources/@frenkelVertexOperatorAlgebras1988.md), §1.6, p. 17
