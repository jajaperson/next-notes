# Ordered pair

An **ordered pair** is a construction satisfying the fundamental property #m/def/set
$$
\begin{align*}
(a_{1},b_{1}) = (a_{2},b_{2}) \iff [a_{1}=a_{2}] \land [b_{1}=b_{2}]
\end{align*}
$$
the set of all ordered pairs from a given pair of sets forms the [cartesian product](Cartesian%20product.md).

## Construction

Within [ZF](ZF.md) the typical model, due to *Kazimierz Kuratowski*, is as follows
$$
\begin{align*}
(a,b) =_{K} \{ \{ a \}, \{ a,b \} \}
\end{align*}
$$
which satisfies the fundamental property.
