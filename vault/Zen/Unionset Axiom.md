# Unionset Axiom

The **Unionset Axiom** is a possible axiom of [Material set theory](Material%20set%20theory.md): #m/def/set/zf
$$
\begin{align*}
(\forall \shood \mathcal{E})(\exists \shood B)[x \in B \iff (\exists X \in \mathcal{E})[x \in X]]
\end{align*}
$$
which is to say, for any set $\mathcal{E}$ there exists a **unionset** $B$ consisting of the elements of the elements of $\mathcal{E}$.
It follows from the [Axiom of Extensionality](Axiom%20of%20Extensionality.md) that such a $B$ is unique, and we denote it by $\bigcup \mathcal{E}$.
