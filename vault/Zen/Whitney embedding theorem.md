# Whitney embedding theorem

The **Whitney embedding theorem** establishes the equivalence of $C^\infty$ [differentiable manifolds](Differentiable%20manifold.md) and [real submanifolds](Real%20embedded%20manifold.md),
as well as placing an upper bound on the Euclidean dimension required to embed a given manifold.
Let $M$ be an $m$-dimensional $C^\infty$ [differentiable manifold](Differentiable%20manifold.md).
Then $M$ is [diffeomorphic](Diffeomorphism.md) to a [real embedded manifold](Real%20embedded%20manifold.md) of $\mathbb{R}^{2m}$,
i.e. $M$ may be smoothly [embedded](Embedding.md) in $\mathbb{R}^{2m}$. #m/thm/geo/diff
