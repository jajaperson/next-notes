# Abelian Lie algebra

A [Lie algebra](Lie%20algebra.md) $\mathfrak{g}$ is said to be **abelian** iff $[\mathfrak{g,\mathfrak{g}}]=0$. #m/def/lie
[Away from 2](Away%20from%202.md) this is the same as requiring the Lie bracket to commute.

## Properties

* Every one-dimensional Lie algebra is abelian.
