# Topology MOC

Topology is the study of the defining features of a *space*.
What makes two spaces the *same*?
[Topology notation in these notes](Topology%20notation%20in%20these%20notes.md)

## Fundamentals

The object of interest is the [Topological space](Topological%20space.md),
which is a space together with a way of defining which subsets are open called the **topology**.

* [Coarseness and fineness of topologies](Coarseness%20and%20fineness%20of%20topologies.md) on the same space
* [Topological basis](Topological%20basis.md), [Topological subbasis](Topological%20subbasis.md)

Open sets allow the definition of a [Neighbourhood](Neighbourhood.md) of a point.

* [Neighbourhood system](Neighbourhood%20system.md), [Neighbourhood basis](Neighbourhood%20basis.md)

## Morphisms

The morphisms of interest are [continuous maps](Continuity.md).
Isomorphisms are then [homeomorphisms](Homeomorphism.md),
which preserve open sets in both directions,
and preserve every [topological property](Topological%20property.md).
Other properties maps can have

* [Open and closed maps](Open%20and%20closed%20maps.md)
* [Proper map](Proper%20map.md)

Special kinds of maps

* [Embedding](Embedding.md)

## Topological properties and axiomatic topology

A [Topological property](Topological%20property.md) is a property which is shared by any two [homeomorphic](Homeomorphism.md) spaces.

* [List of topological properties](Topological%20property.md)
* [Countability axioms](Countability%20axioms.md)
* [Separation axioms](Separation%20axioms.md)
