# Algebra ideal

A [subalgebra](Subalgebra%20over%20a%20field.md)[^subs] $I \leq A$ of an [algebra](Algebra%20over%20a%20field.md) $A$ over a field $\mathbb{K}$ is called a **left-ideal** iff $AI \sube I$,
a **right-ideal** iff $IA \sube I$,
and a **two-sided ideal** (sometimes just **ideal**) iff both conditions hold, #m/def/falg
i.e. a left-ideal absorbs elements placed on the left, &c.
Compare with an [ideal](Ideal.md) of a rng.
Given a two-sided ideal one may construct a [Quotient algebra](Quotient%20algebra.md).

[^subs]: Note that any vector susppace satisfying the definition is automatically a subalgebra.
