# Representation theory MOC

**Representation theory** studies [algebraic structures](Abstract%20algebra%20MOC.md) by representing their elements as [linear maps](Linear%20map.md) of [vector spaces](Vector%20space.md).
In general, a [Representation](Representation.md) is a [Functor](Functor.md) $\Gamma : G \to \Vect_{\mathbb{K}}$.
