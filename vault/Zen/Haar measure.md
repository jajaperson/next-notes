# Haar measure

Given a [locally compact](Locally%20compact%20space.md) [Hausdorff](Hausdorff%20space.md) [topological group](Topological%20group.md) $G$,
there exist [nontrivial](Trivial%20measure.md), [regular](Regular%20measure.md), [locally finite](Locally%20finite%20measure.md), left- and right-invariant [Borel measures](Borel%20set.md) on $G$,
unique up to multiplication by a positive constant,
called the **left Haar measure** $\mu_{L}$ and **right Haar measure** $\mu_{R}$ of $G$ respectively. #m/thm/group
Invariance means given [Borel set](Borel%20set.md) $U \sube G$ and a group element $g \in G$

* $\mu_{L}(U) = \mu_{L}(gU)$
* $\mu_{R}(U) = \mu_{R}(Ug)$

The main use of the Haar measure is analogous to the *Reärrangement lemma* in finite contexts.

## Further terminology

* A [Unimodular group](Unimodular%20group.md) is a group for which $\mu_{L}=\mu_{R} = \mu$.

## Explicit constructions and examples

* *Haar measure of a discrete group* is counting measure
