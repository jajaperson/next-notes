# Topological group

A **topological group** $(G, \cdot, \mathcal{T})$ is a [group](Group.md) $(G, \cdot)$ that is also a [topological space](Topological%20space.md) $(G, \mathcal{T})$,
such that the group operation and taking the inverse is continuous, #m/def/group
i.e. a topological group is a *Group object* in the [Category of topological spaces](Category%20of%20topological%20spaces.md).

## Properties

* Every topological group is a [Homogenous space](Homogenous%20space.md).
* [The connected component of the identity is a normal subgroup](Connected%20subgroup.md).

## Related concepts

* A [Lie group](Lie%20group.md) is a topological group on which one can perform calculus.
