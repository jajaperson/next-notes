# Real embedded manifold

In these notes, a **real embedded manifold** typically refers to a $C^\infty$ [Submanifold](Submanifold.md) of [real coördinate space](Real%20co%C3%B6rdinate%20space.md). #m/def/geo/diff
The [Whitney embedding theorem](Whitney%20embedding%20theorem.md) provides a sense in which every real [differentiable manifold](Differentiable%20manifold.md) may be regarded as a real submanifold.

A subset $X \sube \mathbb{R}^N$ is an $n$-dimensional **real submanifold** iff has [charts](Coo%CC%88rdinate%20chart.md) that are $C^\infty$ [diffeomorphisms as subsets of real coördinate space](Differentiability.md#arbitrary-subsets-of-real-coordinate-space),
i.e. for every $x \in X$
there exists a neighbourhood $U'$ of $x$ in $\mathbb{R}^N$
and an open set $V \sube \mathbb{R}^n$
such that there exists a $C^\infty$ map $\varphi : U' \twoheadrightarrow V$
with a $C^\infty$ right-inverse $\psi = (\varphi \restriction U' \cap X)^{-1}$.
