# Prime ideal

A (two-sided) proper, nontrivial [ideal](Ideal.md) $I \triangleleft R$ is called a **prime ideal** iff for any $a,b \in R$, $ab \in I$ implies $a \in I$ or $b \in I$[^2017], #m/def/ring i.e.
$$
\begin{align*}
ab \in I \iff [a \in I] \lor [b \in I]
\end{align*}
$$
The motivating idea behind this definition are the prime numbers in $\mathbb{Z}$:
An ideal $I \trianglelefteq \mathbb{Z}$ is prime iff $I = p\mathbb{Z}$ where $p$ is prime.

[^2017]: 2017. [Contemporary abstract algebra](../Sources/@gallianContemporaryAbstractAlgebra2017.md), §14, p. 253
