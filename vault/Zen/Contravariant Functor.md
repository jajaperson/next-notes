# Contravariant functor

A contravariant functor (as opposed to a [covariant functor](Functor.md)) behaves very much like a covariant functor,
except for the fact that it *flips morphisms*.
Because of this, a contravariant functor $F$ from $\cat C$ to $\cat D$ is equivalent to a functor from the [Opposite category](Opposite%20category.md) of $\cat C$.
$$
F: \cop C \to \cat D
$$
