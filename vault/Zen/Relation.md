# Relation

A **relation** between [sets](Set.md) $A$ and $B$ is a construct which *relates* elements $a \in A$ or $b\in B$, so that $a \sim b$ is either true or false. #m/def/set
We may therefore define a relation $R$ as the following [subset](Subset.md) of the [cartesian product](Cartesian%20product.md) $A \times B$
$$
\begin{align*}
R = \left\{ (a, b) \in A \times B : a \sim b \right\}
\end{align*}
$$
or equivalently as a function $A \times B \to \mathbb{2}$.
A special class of relation is the [Equivalence relation](Equivalence%20relation.md).
