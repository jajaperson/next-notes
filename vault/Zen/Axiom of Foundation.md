# Axiom of Foundation

The **Axiom of Foundation** or **Axiom of Regularity** is a possible axiom of [Material set theory](Material%20set%20theory.md) first proposed by *John von Neumann*: #m/def/set/zf
$$
\begin{align*}
(\forall \shood x)[ (\exists \shood y)[y \in x] \implies (\exists y \in x)[y \cap x = \0] ]
\end{align*}
$$
which is to say, every set containing at least one set has an element disjoint to itself.
The Axiom of Foundation thus precludes[^w] infinite chains of elementhood and therefore demands that all sets be [well-founded](Well-founded%20set.md) —
in fact, this demand turns out to be equivalent to the Axiom of Foundation.
