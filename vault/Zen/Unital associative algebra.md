# Unital associative algebra

A **unital associative algebra** $A$ is an [algebra over a field](Algebra%20over%20a%20field.md) $\mathbb{K}$ for which the bilinear product possesses a two-sided identity and is associative. #m/def/falg

* $\mathbb{1} a = a\mathbb{1} = a$ for all $a \in A$
* $(ab)c = a(bc)$ for all $a,b,c \in A$

In other words, an associative algebra is a field action on a [ring](Ring.md).
An algebra homomorphism which preserves the identity is a [unital associative algebra homomorphism](Unital%20associative%20algebra%20homomorphism.md).
