# Graded Lie algebra

Let $(\mathfrak{U}, +)$ be a (necessarily abelian) [monoid](Monoid.md).
A $\mathfrak{U}$-**graded Lie algebra** is an example of a [graded algebra](Graded%20algebra.md), #m/def/lie
i.e.
$$
\begin{align*}
[A_{\alpha}, A_{\beta}] \sube A_{\alpha + \beta}
\end{align*}
$$
