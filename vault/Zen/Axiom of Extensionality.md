# Axiom of Extensionality

The **Axiom of Extensionality** is a possible axiom in [Material set theory](Material%20set%20theory.md) that seems to follow directly from [Cantor's definition](Set.md#def) of the [set](Set.md): #m/def/set/zf
$$
\begin{align*}
(\forall \shood A)(\forall \shood B)[A=B \iff (\forall x )[x \in A \iff x \in B]]
\end{align*}
$$
which is to say, two sets are the same iff they have the same elements.

## Relation to other axioms

* The Axiom of Extensionality seems to give a complete definition of set equality, but if we are dealing with [ill-founded](Well-founded%20set.md) sets it may reduce to tautologous $A = B \iff A = B$. Thus Extensionality is compatible with *Aczel's Antifoundation Axiom*.
