# Homogenous space

A **homogenous space** $X$ is a [topological space](Topological%20space.md) such that for any $x,y \in X$ there exists an automorphism $f : X \to X$ such thar $f(x)=y$. #m/def/topology
Thus the space ‘looks the same’ everywhere.

## Homogeneity under an action

A topological space $X$ is homogeneous under a [group action](Group%20action.md) $\alpha : G \times X \to X$ if for all $x,y \in X$ there exists a $g \in G$ such that $\alpha(g,x) = y$. #m/def/topology
