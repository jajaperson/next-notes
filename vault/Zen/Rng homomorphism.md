# Rng homomorphism

A **rng homomorphism** is a [morphism](Morphism.md) in the *Category of rngs*,
that is to say a structure-preserving map between [rngs](Rng.md). #m/def/ring
Let $A,B$ be rngs and let $f : A \to B$.
Then $f$ is a homomorphism iff for any $x,y \in A$

1. $f(a + b) = f(a) + f(b)$
1. $f(ab)=f(a)f(b)$

that is to say $f$ is a [homomorphism](Group%20homomorphism.md) of both the additive group and the multiplicative monoid.
For unital rings, see [Ring homomorphism](Ring%20homomorphism.md).
