# Semisimple module

A [module](Module.md) $M \in \lMod R$ is **semisimple** or **completely reducible** iff it is the [direct sum](Direct%20sum%20of%20modules.md) of [simple](Simple%20module.md) [submodules](Submodule.md). #m/def/module
