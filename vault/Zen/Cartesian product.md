# Cartesian product

The **cartesian product** $A \times B$ is the set of all [ordered pairs](Ordered%20pair.md) $(a,b)$ where $a \in A$ and $B \in B$. #m/def/set
This is the [categorical product](Products%20and%20coproducts.md) in the *category of sets*.
