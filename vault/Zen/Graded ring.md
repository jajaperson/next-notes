# Graded ring

Let $(\mathfrak{U},+)$ be a [monoid](Monoid.md).
A [ring](Ring.md) $R$ is said to be $\mathfrak{U}$-**graded** if its additive group $R^+$ is the [direct sum of abelian groups](Direct%20product%20of%20groups.md) $R_{\alpha}$ indexed by $\alpha \in \mathfrak{U}$
such that $R_{\alpha} \cdot R_{\beta} \sube R_{\alpha+\beta}$ for any $\alpha,\beta \in \mathfrak{U}$. #m/def/ring
Typically $\mathfrak{U} = \mathbb{Z}$ or $M = \mathbb{N}_{0}$, but in principle any monoid can be used.
