# Abelian group

A [group](Group.md) $A$ is said to be **abelian** if all its elements commute. #m/def/group
The same terminology can also be applied to a commutative [Monoid](Monoid.md),
but is rarely used outside of these algebraic objects.

## Properties

* An abelian group has the same structure as a $\mathbb{Z}$-module. See *Abelian groups as ℤ-modules*.
* An abelian group is equal to its [centre](Centre%20of%20a%20group.md), as well as the [centraliser](Centralizer%20of%20a%20group%20element.md) of every element.
* [Irreps of abelian groups are 1-dimensional](Irreps%20of%20abelian%20groups%20are%201-dimensional.md)
* [Kernel of a homomorphism into an abelian group](Kernel%20of%20a%20homomorphism%20into%20an%20abelian%20group.md)
* All subgroups of an abelian group are [normal](Normal%20subgroup.md)
