# Regular representation

The **regular representation** $\Lambda : A \to \End(A)$ of an [Unital associative algebra](Unital%20associative%20algebra.md) $A$ is a natural [Representation](Representation.md) of $A$ carried by itself, where
$$
\begin{align*}
\Lambda(a)b = a b
\end{align*}
$$
Thus every [Unital associative algebra](Unital%20associative%20algebra.md) is a [module](Module%20over%20a%20unital%20associative%20algebra.md) over itself.
