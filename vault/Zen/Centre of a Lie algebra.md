# Centre of a Lie algebra

The **centre** $\mathfrak{z} (\mathfrak{g})$ of a [Lie algebra](Lie%20algebra.md) $\mathfrak{g}$ is a [Lie algebra ideal](Lie%20algebra%20ideal.md) consisting of elements which annihilate all elements in the Lie bracket, #m/def/lie i.e.
$$
\begin{align*}
\mathfrak{z} (\mathfrak{g}) = \{ x \in \mathfrak{ g} : [x, \mathfrak{g}] = \{ 0 \} \} = \ker \ad_{(-)}
\end{align*}
$$
It is the [kernel](Kernel%20of%20a%20Lie%20algebra%20homomorphism.md) of the [adjoint Lie algebra representation](Adjoint%20Lie%20algebra%20representation.md)
and thus an [ideal](Lie%20algebra%20ideal.md) of $\mathfrak{g}$.[^alt]

[^alt]: It is otherwise easy to convince oneself that this is an ideal, since it “absorbs” all elements by sending them to zero.

## Properties

* Any linear subspace of $\mathfrak{z}(\mathfrak{g})$ is an ideal of $\mathfrak{g}$, called a [Central ideal](Central%20ideal.md).
