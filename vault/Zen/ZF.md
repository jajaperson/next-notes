# Zermelo-Fraenkel set theory

**Zermelo-Fraenkel set theory** or $\mathrm{ZF}$ is a [Material set theory](Material%20set%20theory.md) and the most widely accepted [Axiomatic set theory](Axiomatic%20set%20theory.md) (together with the stronger [ZFC](ZFC.md)).

## Axioms

We take the following axioms and axiom schemata: #m/def/set/zf

1. [Axiom of Extensionality](Axiom%20of%20Extensionality.md)
1. [Emptyset Axiom](Emptyset%20Axiom.md)
1. [Axiom of Pairing](Axiom%20of%20Pairing.md)
1. [Unionset Axiom](Unionset%20Axiom.md)
1. [Separation Axiom Schema](Separation%20Axiom%20Schema.md)
1. [Powerset Axiom](Powerset%20Axiom.md)
1. [Axiom of Infinity](Axiom%20of%20Infinity.md)
1. [Axiom Schema of Replacement](Axiom%20Schema%20of%20Replacement.md)
1. [Axiom of Foundation](Axiom%20of%20Foundation.md)
