# Associative algebra homomorphism

A **unital associative algebra homomorphism** is a [unital algebra homomorphism](Algebra%20homomorphism.md) of [associative algebras](Unital%20associative%20algebra.md), #m/def/falg
i.e. $f:A\to B$ such that for $x,y \in A$ and $\lambda,\mu \in \mathbb{K}$

1. $f(\lambda x+\mu y) = \lambda f(x)+\mu f(y)$
1. $f(xy)= f(x)f(y)$
1. $f(1_{A})=1_{B}$

as such, an associative algebra homomorphism is also a [ring homomorphism](Ring%20homomorphism.md).
